# Section Prompt: Financial Projections

## Structure

### 13.1 Projected P&L Statement
Table: Particulars | Actual (Latest FY) | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Source
- Project revenue, EBITDA, depreciation, interest, PBT, PAT for 3 years
- Growth based on historical CAGR (capped at 25% p.a.)
- Show EBITDA margin and PAT margin projections

### 13.2 Cash Flow & DSCR Projections
Table: Particulars | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Source
- Project Operating Cash Flow, debt repayment, interest outflow
- Compute DSCR for each projected year
- Flag years where DSCR < 1.25x

### 13.3 DSRA Calculation
Table: Component | Value | Source
- Calculate 1-quarter interest + principal reserve
- Calculate 6-month interest reserve
- Show recommended DSRA amount

### 13.4 Sensitivity Analysis
Table: Scenario | Revenue Impact | EBITDA | DSCR | Assessment | Source
- Base Case
- Revenue decline by 10%
- Operating cost increase by 10%
- Combined stress scenario

## Source Attribution
- "Projected (based on historical CAGR)" for projections
- "Bank Credit Model" for DSCR computations
- "Stress Testing Framework" for sensitivity analysis
