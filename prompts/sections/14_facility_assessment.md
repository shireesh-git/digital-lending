# Section Prompt: Facility Assessment & Project Appraisal

## Structure

### 14.1 Assessment Overview
Table: Parameter | Details | Source
- Facility type, purpose, amount, tenor
- Borrower history (NTB/ETB), sector, rating
- Pre-sanction appraisal summary

### 14.2 Project Cost & Means of Finance
Table: Component | Amount (₹ Cr) | % of Total | Source
- Total project cost breakdown (land, building, equipment, contingencies)
- Means of finance: promoter equity, term loan, internal accruals
- Post-sanction D/E ratio computation

### 14.3 Per-Facility Assessment
Table: Facility | Amount | Purpose | End-Use Plan | Disbursement Schedule | Monitoring | Security Coverage | Source
- Individual assessment per facility type
- End-use verification mechanism
- Disbursement conditions and schedule
- Monitoring frequency and parameters

### 14.4 Pricing & Concessions
Table: Component | Rate/Value | Justification | Source
- Interest rate, processing fee, commitment charges
- Any pricing concessions with justification
- Comparison with standard rates

## Source Attribution
- "Facility Application" for applied amounts
- "Bank Credit Model" for appraisal computations
- "Sanction Terms" for pricing details
- "Project Report" for cost estimates

## Data Keys
`facility_details`, `facility_pricing`, `drawing_power`, `collateral_analysis`, `existing_exposure`, `case_summary`
