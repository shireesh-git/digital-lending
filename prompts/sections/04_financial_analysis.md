# Section Prompt: Financial Analysis

Write a comprehensive Financial Analysis with deep analytical commentary and decision-impact assessment.

## 4.1 Profit & Loss Statement (₹ Cr)
Table: Particulars | FY20XX | FY20XX | FY20XX
- Revenue, EBITDA, EBITDA Margin %, Depreciation, Finance Cost, PBT, Tax, PAT, PAT Margin %
- **YoY Growth Table:** Period | Revenue | YoY Growth %
- **CAGR** across the analysis period (if 3+ years available)
- Revenue trajectory analysis — what's driving growth/decline (volume vs. price, segment mix, geographic expansion)
- Margin analysis — operating leverage, raw material cost trend, employee cost intensity, efficiency improvements
- Profitability sustainability assessment — is the margin trajectory maintainable?

## 4.2 Balance Sheet Summary (₹ Cr)
Table: Net Worth, Tangible Net Worth, Total Debt (LT + ST breakdown), Total Assets, Current Assets, Current Liabilities,
Trade Receivables, Inventory, Trade Payables, Cash & Equivalents
- **Leverage Analysis:** D/E ratio trend with interpretation (conservative <1x, moderate 1-2x, aggressive >2x)
- **Liquidity Assessment:** Current ratio and quick ratio with adequacy commentary
- **Working Capital Position:** Net working capital and % of revenue
- **Capital Structure Commentary:** debt mix (LT vs. ST), funding maturity profile, reliance on short-term debt
- **Net Worth Quality:** retained earnings vs. capital infusion, intangible assets impact on TNW

## 4.3 Cash Flow & Repayment Capacity (₹ Cr)
Table: Operating Cash Flow, Capital Expenditure, Free Cash Flow, Dividends, Net Cash Flow
- **Cash Quality Ratio:** OCF/PAT — strong (>1.2x), adequate (0.5-1.2x), weak (<0.5x)
- **Capex Intensity:** Capex as % of revenue — growth capex vs. maintenance capex assessment
- **FCF Available for Debt Servicing:** surplus after capex and working capital changes
- **Repayment Capacity Table:** Annual EBITDA | Finance Cost | Principal Repayment | Surplus/(Deficit) | DSCR
- **Debt Servicing Headroom:** margin of safety analysis — how much can EBITDA decline before DSCR < 1.0x

## 4.4 Key Financial Ratios
Comprehensive table with borrower values across periods + peer benchmark median:
| Ratio | FY20XX | FY20XX | FY20XX | Peer Median | Position |
- Current Ratio, Quick Ratio, D/E, TOL/TNW, Debt/EBITDA, ICR, DSCR
- EBITDA Margin, PAT Margin, ROE, ROA, ROCE
- Asset Turnover, Fixed Asset Turnover
- Debtor Days, Inventory Days, Payable Days, Working Capital Cycle (Cash Conversion Cycle)

### Working Capital Analysis
- Debtor days trend — improving/deteriorating collection efficiency
- Inventory days — stock holding period and its implications
- Payable days — supplier payment terms strength
- Cash conversion cycle — improvement trajectory and sector comparison

### DuPont Decomposition (if data available)
ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
Identify which lever is driving ROE changes.

## 4.5 Decision Impact Analysis
For each key financial metric, provide structured assessment:

| Metric | Current Value | Trend | vs. Peer | Credit Impact | Severity |
|--------|--------------|-------|----------|---------------|----------|

Explain:
1. What the number means for credit quality (e.g., "D/E of 0.8x indicates conservative leverage")
2. Whether it's improving, stable, or deteriorating (with specific YoY movement)
3. How it compares vs. peer benchmarks (above/in-line/below)
4. The impact on risk grade and recommendation (positive/neutral/negative factor)
5. If negative: what mitigating factors exist
