# Section Prompt: Detailed Operational Analysis & Industry KPIs

## Structure

### 12.1 KPI Benchmarking Table
Table: KPI Metric | External Benchmark (AI) | Internal Benchmark (Bank) | Company Value | Source / Remarks
- Sector-specific KPIs (Infrastructure: project cost/km, book-to-bill, equipment utilization; Manufacturing: capacity utilization, raw material %; Pharma: R&D spend, ANDA pipeline; IT: revenue/employee, attrition)
- Financial KPIs: EBITDA margin, D/E ratio, DSCR, ICR, ROE
- Working capital KPIs: Debtor days, inventory days, WC cycle

### 12.2 KPI Assessment & Commentary
- Compare company performance vs external sector benchmarks and internal bank norms
- Flag KPIs that deviate significantly from benchmarks
- Provide sector-specific operational insights
- Summarize overall operational efficiency position

## Source Attribution
Every row must include Source / Remarks showing data origin:
- "Audited Financials" for company-derived values
- "CMIE / Industry Database" for external benchmarks
- "Bank Credit Policy" for internal benchmarks
- "Management Disclosed" for operational data

## Data Keys
`infra_metrics`, `industry_analysis`, `benchmark_summary`, `financial_summary`, `ratio_analysis`, `case_summary`
